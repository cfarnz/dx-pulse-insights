# Sample Pulse Survey — Open-Text Responses

Simulated open-text responses to a quarterly developer experience pulse survey question:
"What's one thing that made your work easier or harder this month?" 18 responses, a mix of
clean positive signal, planted friction, and a couple of ambiguous/off-topic ones — a
realistic queue, not a curated one.

## RESPONSE 1: Backend, mid-level
CI takes almost 25 minutes on the main pipeline now. I kick it off and go do something else,
then forget to come back and check, so my PR just sits there overnight sometimes.

## RESPONSE 2: Frontend, senior
Code review turnaround has actually been great this quarter — usually get a first pass
within a couple hours. Whoever pushed for the review SLA, thank you.

## RESPONSE 3: Platform, senior
I spent most of Tuesday just trying to figure out who owns the billing service now after the
reorg. Docs still list the old team. Once I found the right person it took ten minutes to
get an answer.

## RESPONSE 4: Backend, junior
Honestly the hardest part of my week is just remembering which of our six internal tools
does what. Feels like I'm holding a map in my head instead of writing code.

## RESPONSE 5: Mobile, mid-level
Nothing major to report, pretty normal month.

## RESPONSE 6: Data, senior
I got two full uninterrupted mornings this month because our team started blocking
"no-meeting Tuesdays." First time in a year I actually finished something in one sitting
instead of picking it back up three times.

## RESPONSE 7: Frontend, mid-level
Between standup, three recurring syncs, and ad hoc Slack huddles, I probably get 90 minutes
of real heads-down time on a good day. It's rare I get into a real flow before something
pulls me out.

## RESPONSE 8: Backend, senior
Our test suite flakes about 1 in 5 runs, so I've started just re-running failures without
looking closely, which honestly isn't great but I don't have time to actually fix flaky
tests and also ship my own work.

## RESPONSE 9: Platform, mid-level
Nothing to add.

## RESPONSE 10: Backend, junior
The new internal CLI docs are so much better than they used to be — actual examples instead
of just a flag list. Ramping onto the deploy tooling took me an afternoon instead of a week.

## RESPONSE 11: Mobile, senior
Approvals for App Store releases are stuck behind three separate sign-offs and it's not
clear who's supposed to go first. Last release sat "ready" for four days waiting on
someone to notice it needed their stamp.

## RESPONSE 12: Data, mid-level
I like my team. The work itself is interesting.

## RESPONSE 13: Frontend, junior
I get pulled into unplanned bug triage almost every day, usually mid-task. By the time I
get back to what I was actually doing I've lost the thread and have to re-read my own code
to remember where I was.

## RESPONSE 14: Backend, mid-level
Local dev environment setup for new hires still takes about two days and involves a
seventeen-step wiki page that's half out of date. I had to ask three different people to get
mine working.

## RESPONSE 15: Platform, senior
Deploys are fast and reliable now — genuinely can't remember the last time a release process
issue cost me time. Big improvement from a year ago.

## RESPONSE 16: Frontend, senior
Design handoff is a black box. I don't find out a spec changed until QA flags a mismatch,
which means I'm reworking things I thought were done.

## RESPONSE 17: Data, junior
Everything's fine, no complaints this month.

## RESPONSE 18: Backend, senior
We finally consolidated three separate logging tools into one. I used to have to check two
or three dashboards to debug a single incident; now it's one place. Small thing but it adds
up every single day.
